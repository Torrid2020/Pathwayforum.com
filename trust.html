Sources and transparency

How we check information

Official sources and dated checks

Scholarship listings link to the provider. Job feeds collect opportunities from the sources shown on each listing. Listings include source and check information where available. Automated checks can miss changes; always confirm eligibility, deadlines and application instructions with the original provider.

Uganda job levels and ordering

Jobs in Uganda, focused on entry and mid-level roles, with senior positions also listed. Levels are inferred from titles, experience requirements and descriptions; they are not employer certifications. Explicit senior or management titles take precedence. Otherwise, six or more required years is senior, three to five is mid level, and zero to two is entry level. Internship and trainee titles are labelled separately. Without experience evidence, clear officer, assistant, consultant, associate, representative, worker, operator, clerk and technician titles are treated as entry level. Specialist, coordinator, analyst II and supervisor titles are treated as mid level. Unclear titles remain “Not stated”.

Internship, entry, mid-level and unstated roles appear first, ordered by the nearest closing date; senior and management roles follow in their own section. Undated roles appear last within each group. Expired vacancies are removed. The homepage counts every listed Uganda role but shows only entry and mid-level examples. Always check the full official advert.

Before you apply

Check the official organisation and recruitment domain. Be cautious of payment requests, guaranteed awards and pressure to act immediately. Pathway Forum does not collect application fees or make hiring decisions.

  • Upfront fees of any kind, whatever they are called: processing, guarantee, redemption or insurance fees.
  • Guarantees of winning. No one can promise you an award before you apply.
  • Requests for bank or card details to deposit an award.
  • Awards you never applied for. Treat unexpected winning messages with caution.
  • Pressure to act immediately or keep things secret.

If you are unsure, find the official website yourself instead of using links in messages, and contact the provider directly.
