BYU-Pathway

Is a BYU-Pathway degree recognised?

In the United States, yes: BYU-Pathway degrees are awarded by accredited universities. Outside the US, recognition depends on the country and on what you want the degree for, so it's worth checking before you rely on it for a job, visa, or master's application.

Last reviewed September 26, 2026

Who awards the degree

This is the most important fact to understand. BYU-Pathway itself does not issue your degree. Your certificates and degrees are developed and awarded by BYU-Idaho and Ensign College, with BYU-Pathway providing online access and student support.

BYU-Pathway says its online programs follow the same curriculum and standards as the on-campus programs of those schools.

Accreditation

The programs are approved by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU), a US regional accreditor. That approval covers the 90–96 credit bachelor's degrees introduced in April 2024.

When a university or employer asks whether your degree is accredited, name the awarding institution (BYU-Idaho or Ensign College) and its accreditor. That's what they'll look up.

Recognition outside the United States

There's no single worldwide answer. Each country decides how to treat foreign qualifications, and universities often make their own decisions on top of that. When an evaluation body compares your degree, it typically considers:

  • the entry requirements of your program,
  • what you studied,
  • how long the program was.

Degree length matters. A 90–96 credit bachelor's is shorter than the roughly 120-credit bachelor's that's typical in the US. Some universities abroad may assess it differently from a four-year degree. Check each programme's entry requirements, and ask admissions directly if they're unclear.

How to get a clear answer for your situation

  1. For a master's abroad: check the university's international entry requirements for US qualifications, then email admissions with your degree name, credit total, and awarding institution.
  2. For the UK: UK ENIC offers a Statement of Comparability, which compares your qualification against the UK education system.
  3. For jobs at home: ask your national education ministry or qualifications authority whether foreign degrees need to be formally evaluated or registered.
  4. For employers generally: have your official transcript ready and be prepared to explain the certificates you earned along the way. They show concrete skills.
