My story and purpose

Why I built Pathway Forum

Through Rise & Reasons, I talk about BYU-Pathway and the questions students face while trying to make good decisions about education, technology and work.

I created Pathway Forum as the practical companion to those videos. It gives students a place to read detailed guides, compare options, find scholarships and discover verified job opportunities without having to search through many unreliable websites.

My goal is simple: explain complicated information clearly and help students move from understanding their education to taking useful action.

1

Clear explanations

I want students to understand their choices without confusing language or unnecessary complexity.

2

Useful opportunities

I focus on scholarships, official resources and jobs that can help students make real progress.

3

Honest independence

I clearly separate my independent guidance from the institutions and employers I discuss.
