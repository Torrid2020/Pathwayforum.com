The person behind Pathway Forum

Hi, I'm James. I've walked the BYU-Pathway path, and I'm still on it.

I'm James Ocora from Kampala, Uganda, the creator of the Rise & Reasons YouTube channel and Pathway Forum.

Why I started Rise & Reasons

I started Rise & Reasons to share my own experience with other students, so they can overcome the challenges that come with studying online and find work online.

My Pathway journey

I started PathwayConnect in 2020. From there I followed the certificate-first path: two certificates in community health and one in epidemiology, an associate degree in Applied Health (2023), and a Bachelor of Science in Applied Health (2025) from BYU–Idaho, all studied online from Uganda.

Today I'm continuing with business studies through BYU–Idaho and Ensign College.

Every step explained on this site is one I've taken myself, usually while working.

What I've learned helping students

Since 2022 I've worked remotely as a student mentor, helping students set goals, plan for success, find the right resources, and keep going through difficult circumstances. I've seen how often good students get stuck simply because information is scattered or confusing.

Why I care about jobs and opportunities

As a human resource manager, I advertised positions and recruited staff, so I know what employers look for. I've also worked in customer service and online sales while studying. That's why Pathway Forum lists only opportunities from official sources, and why you should never pay to apply for a job or scholarship.

Why I built Pathway Forum

Videos are great for explaining, but students also need something they can come back to. Pathway Forum is where you keep what you need: clear guides, checked scholarship deadlines and verified opportunities, in one place.

What you can expect

Clear explanations

I want students to understand their choices without confusing language or unnecessary complexity.

Useful opportunities

I focus on scholarships, official resources and jobs that can help students make real progress.

Honest independence

I clearly separate my independent guidance from the institutions and employers I discuss.
