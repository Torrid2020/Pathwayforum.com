Privacy policy

What Pathway Forum collects, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who runs this site

Pathway Forum is an independent resource run by James Ocora and based in Uganda. We handle personal information in line with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019: collecting only what is needed, explaining its use, and respecting requests to access, correct or remove it.

No accounts

You do not need an account to read guides or browse jobs. We do not ask you to upload a CV or send job applications to Pathway Forum. Apply links take you to the employer or credited job service, which has its own privacy policy.

Email alerts

Email sign-ups are not open yet. The current form does not save or send your email address. Once sign-ups launch, we will collect your email address only when you choose to subscribe, and use it for the alerts you request. Brevo is the planned email service. You will be able to unsubscribe through an email link or by contacting us. We do not sell subscriber email addresses.

Employer submissions and messages

Employer submissions and reports arrive by email. We use the vacancy details and any contact information you provide to review the advert, respond, and handle corrections or removal requests. Please do not send applicant CVs, identity documents or other sensitive personal information. We keep messages only as long as needed for these purposes.

Saved jobs stay in your browser

Saved jobs are stored in your own browser on your device. They are not sent to a Pathway Forum account or synced between devices. You can remove saved jobs on the site or clear this site’s browser storage.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Your browser and the hosting provider exchange technical information, such as your IP address, to load pages. External services, including YouTube thumbnails and any linked platforms you visit, may also receive technical information and process it under their own policies. An email service may process subscriber information outside Uganda when sign-ups launch.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

Google AdSense and analytics are planned; this policy does not switch them on. If we introduce them, we will update this page and provide cookie choices and consent where required. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits or personalise advertising. Job detail pages do not show ads.

You can manage personalised Google ads in Google’s Ad Settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Turning off personalised ads does not necessarily remove all advertising. Saved-job browser storage is separate from advertising cookies.

Your choices and contact

To ask what information we hold, correct it, request removal, unsubscribe or ask a privacy question, email james.torrid1805@gmail.com. Include only the information needed to identify your request. We respond to requests and remove information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.
